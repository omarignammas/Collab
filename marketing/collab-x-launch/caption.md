## Main post

We built Collab after repeating this loop:

Team chat → AI tab → repeat the context → copy → paste.

Why wasn’t the AI already in the room?

Now `Ctrl + Alt + C` opens Collab above your work. Ask once, focus together, and leave with a shared report.

Building it in public.

## First reply

The original moment was painfully simple: Ayoub and I were choosing a stack and a free deployment path for our sales app. I kept leaving our chat to ask another AI, then carrying the answer back by hand.

Inside a Collab Focus Room, the team can ask the question once. Collab uses the shared context, prepares the decision report, and keeps the result with the project.

When the room ends, it also turns the conversation into decisions, notes, questions, and assigned next steps.

No tab switching. No lost context. Just focused execution and shared clarity.
