Your team does not need more tabs.

While building Collab, we kept running into the same problem: useful work was spread across chat, AI tools, tasks, notes, timers and reports. Every answer required another context switch, and every session ended with decisions that were difficult to find later.

So we designed Collab around one continuous workflow:

1. Open the desktop widget from anywhere.
2. Ask naturally by text or voice.
3. Focus with the team inside a shared room.
4. Keep the AI inside the conversation.
5. Turn the session into a sourced report with clear next actions.
6. Understand where focused time actually went.

The goal is not to add another productivity dashboard. It is to remove the distance between deciding, focusing and executing.

Less switching. More momentum.

We are building Collab in public.
